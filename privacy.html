Privacy Policy

Last updated: 19 October 2026. Kai's Catch is made by KRM Studio ("we"). This policy explains what we collect when you play Kai's Catch and why.

What we collect

  • A random player ID created when you first open the game (no name needed). It lets us keep your cloud save and purchases tied to you.
  • Your email address, only if you choose to create an account with email or Google. We use it to sign you in and to restore your progress.
  • Game progress (coins, levels, catches, outfits) so you can continue on a new phone.
  • Purchase records from Google Play, processed by RevenueCat, so we can deliver gems and the Captain's Club.
  • Advertising ID, used by Google AdMob to show ads to players who have not removed them. If you decline personalized ads in the consent form, only non-personalized ads are shown.
  • Basic app events (for example "level reached") without personal details, to improve the game.

What we do not collect

We do not collect your contacts, photos, precise location, microphone or camera data. We do not sell your data.

Who processes it

  • Supabase (our database and sign-in provider)
  • RevenueCat and Google Play (purchases and subscriptions)
  • Google AdMob (ads, with your consent where required)

Children

Kai's Catch is designed for a general audience aged 13 and up and is not directed at children under 13.

Keeping and deleting your data

You can delete your account and all cloud data anytime in the game: Settings, Account, Delete account. Purchase records may be kept as long as the law requires. Subscriptions must be cancelled in Google Play.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR) you can ask to access, correct or delete your data. Contact us and we will reply within 30 days.
